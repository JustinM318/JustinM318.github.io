Warehouse Management Modernization

Enterprise software / Current role

My current job. I lead a complete modernization of the core warehouse management system (WMS) that runs 20+ client sites for GM, Nissan and BMW. Its legacy VB.NET business logic moves to a C# Web API, and the front end is being rewritten from scratch in React.

The catch is that it all happens against live operations. These warehouses feed automotive assembly lines just-in-sequence: parts arrive in the exact order the line builds cars, so a sequencing error stops production. The migration ships incrementally, one piece at a time, with no disruption to active sites.

GameCorner / KUA Bot

Python / Vue / Discord + Web

A Discord bot and an installable mobile web app that share one database and one economy. A play-money casino, a trading card game, auctions and a sportsbook. Play on Discord or on your phone with a single balance and collection.

It runs for a private Discord community, so most people will never get to play it. Everything on this page was recorded from the real app.

In motion

Screens

On Discord

How I built it

One balance, two front ends. The hardest constraint was that a player's caps and cards must be identical whether they play in Discord or in the app. Two databases that sync would eventually drift, so I didn't build that. The bot owns the economy, and the app changes it only through a small authenticated internal API. That covers every bet, payout, pack and transfer. Game math is vendored into the app so it can render instantly. A parity test fails CI if the two copies ever disagree, and a contract test pins the shape of every request between the two services.

Auctions that can't double-spend. Listing a card takes it out of the seller's binder, and placing a bid holds the bidder's caps. Both sides are escrowed, so settlement can never find the card or the money already spent elsewhere. Outbid players are refunded right away, and unsold cards come back. The background job that settles auctions claims each one with a guarded UPDATE before acting, so two overlapping passes can't settle the same auction twice. Raises have to beat the current bid by 5% or 10 caps, which stops +1 bidding wars.

Shipping without breaking a live economy. Real people have real balances, so nothing goes straight to production. Feature branches deploy to a separate staging stack in the cloud. There, a headless copy of the bot's API runs against a snapshot of the database, so I can test bets and pack openings on my phone without touching real caps. Merging to main runs CI and redeploys the production containers. Patch notes are written as part of the change and posted to players automatically.

BBB AUTO LINE Claims Portal

Decisions (low-code) / Client project for BBB National Programs / Live site ↗

As a consultant, I built the public landing page and the claim-filing form for BBB National Programs' Dispute Resolution portal. Consumers use it to file vehicle warranty and lemon-law disputes with participating manufacturers through BBB AUTO LINE, free of charge and without an attorney.

It's built on Decisions, a low-code workflow platform. The form walks a claimant through qualifying questions, contact details, vehicle information, the claim itself and a review step, then hands the result off to the BBB AUTO LINE team.

Screens

What I built

Landing page

One entry point for three programs: AUTO LINE, class-action arbitration, and contract terms-of-service disputes.

Guided claim form

A multi-step wizard with a progress tracker, so a long legal intake reads as a handful of short questions.

Qualifying logic

Up-front questions screen out claims that aren't covered warranty disputes before the claimant spends time on details.

Business impact

Made it easier for consumers to get their warranties honored, and supported customer relations by sending cleaner, complete claims to the team.
